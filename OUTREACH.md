Here's your free demo: [LINK]

```

2. Services you offer (names, prices, duration)
3. Your business hours

I'll have it ready in 24 hours.
```

```
It's $29/month for the Professional plan:
- Unlimited bookings

```

**If they don't respond:**
```
Hi [Name], just checking in. The free demo is still available if you're interested in online booking for [Business Name].
```

## Where to Find Businesses

1. **Instagram** - Search hashtags like #salon, #barbershop, #clinic + city name
2. **Google Maps** - Search "salons near me" or "clinics in [city]"
3. **Yelp** - Find businesses with bad/no websites
4. **Facebook** - Local business groups
5. **TikTok** - Business accounts in your niche

## Conversion Funnel

- 60 DMs sent
- 12 replies (20%)
- 6 interested (10%)
- 3 close (5%)
- **3 new clients per week**

At $29/month = $87/month recurring
At $29/month × 12 months = $1,044/year per client
3 clients/week × 4 weeks = 12 clients/month
